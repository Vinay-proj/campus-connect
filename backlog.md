# Campus Connect - Product Backlog

1. As a student, I want to register with my ID, name, and department.
2. As a student, I want to see a list of all registered students.
3. As an admin, I want to search for a student quickly, even with many records.
4. As a student, I want to see upcoming campus events.
5. As a student, I want to use a simple webpage instead of a black terminal window.
